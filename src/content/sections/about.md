Before starting my PhD, I completed my Master's degree in Cybersecurity at ETH
Zurich and EPFL with a
[thesis on the Threema encrypted messenger](https://breakingthe3ma.app). I also
hold a Bachelor's degree in Computing Engineering from the
[Polytechnic University of Milan (Politecnico di Milano)](https://polimi.it).

I (used to) play CTFs as part of various teams, including
[Tower of Hanoi](https://towerofhanoi.it/),
[Mhackeroni](https://mhackeroni.it/), [Flagbot](https://flagbot.ch), and
[Organizers](https://org.anize.rs/) and I was part of the 2021 and 2022 rosters
for [Team Italy](https://teamitaly.eu/). I was captain of the Politecnico di
Milano team in the national [CyberChallenge.IT](https://cyberchallenge.it/)
competition in 2020, reaching national 2nd place.

I enjoy running, swimming, cycling, hiking in the beautiful Swiss mountains,
trying out new foods, trying out specialty cafes, and I sometimes like to
subject myself to the pain of web development.
